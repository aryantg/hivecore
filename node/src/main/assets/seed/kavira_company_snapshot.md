# Kavira Pharmaceuticals: company snapshot

Kavira Pharmaceuticals Pvt Ltd is a fictional small enterprise with about INR 60 crore of revenue and 280 employees. Its headquarters and plant are in Bengaluru, with branches in Bengaluru, Chennai and Kochi. It sells 16 brands and also does government tender supply and contract manufacturing for other companies. The company was founded in 2006 and holds 204 company-issued Android phones.

## Net sales by fiscal year

| Fiscal year | Net sales | Growth | What happened |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FY2023 (baseline) | INR 41.9 Cr | - | Two branches |
| FY2024 | INR 43.1 Cr | +2.8 % | Pantowin price cap, lost contract client A, Chennai flooding; Kochi opens |
| FY2025 | INR 49.6 Cr | +15.1 % | Tamil Nadu tender, Vildora M launch, Kochi ramps up |
| FY2026 | INR 59.5 Cr | +19.9 % | Acneclear launch, new contract client C, Kochi at full strength, despite a metformin shortage |

The Indian fiscal year runs from April to March and is named by the year it ends in: FY2024 is April 2023 to March 2024. FY2023 is a baseline year only.

## Business events

**2023-04, Pantowin price ceiling (pricing).** Government price ceiling on pantoprazole 40 mg cuts Pantowin trade price ~20%; FY2024 Pantowin sales fall ~12%.

**2023-07, Kochi branch opens (expansion).** Kochi branch opens for Kerala: 12 medical reps by Mar 2024, 19 by Mar 2025, 24 by Mar 2026. Set-up cost INR 40 lakh in Q2 FY2024.

**2023-12, Chennai flooding (disruption).** Cyclone-related flooding disrupts Chennai depot dispatches; Chennai December sales down ~20%.

**2024-01, Client A contract ends (contract mfg).** Client A moves production in-house; ~INR 3.8 Cr a year of contract revenue lost.

**2024-04, Online pharmacy partnerships (channel).** Distribution through two online pharmacy partners expanded.

**2024-08, Vildora M launched (launch).** Vildagliptin + metformin launched in all three branches; dedicated tender manager hired in Chennai.

**2024-09, Tamil Nadu tender won (tender).** 18-month state supply contract (Oct 2024 - Mar 2026) for paracetamol, azithromycin, cefixime and metformin; ~INR 8.3 Cr total.

**2025-04, Dermatology product manager (marketing).** Product manager hired to lead the dermatology range ahead of Acneclear.

**2025-06, Acneclear Gel launched (launch).** Adapalene + clindamycin gel launched; strongest in Bengaluru.

**2025-07, Client C signed (contract mfg).** New contract manufacturing client; 8 production operators hired; validation batches cost INR 25 lakh in Q2 FY2026.

**2025-11, Metformin shortage (supply).** Raw-material shortage cuts Metfora, Glimora and Vildora supply ~28% in Nov-Dec 2025; tender supply protected.

**2026-02, WHO-GMP certification (quality).** Bengaluru plant receives WHO-GMP certification, opening export tenders from FY2027.

**2026-03, Hyderabad branch approved (corporate).** Board approves a fourth branch in Hyderabad for FY2027.

## How to read the data

Sales figures are net sales in rupees after discounts. Units are packs sold; for contract manufacturing they are thousands of tablets. Segments are Branded Formulations, Institutional, Contract Manufacturing. Contract manufacturing is billed from the Bengaluru plant. There is no data after March 2026, no Hyderabad branch yet, no competitor data and no former employees.
